# Acme Notes Acceptable Use Policy

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs all use of the Acme Notes service. By accessing or using Acme Notes, the customer agrees to this AUP. Violations may result in suspension or termination of access.

## Prohibited Content

Customers may not upload, store, share, or transmit through Acme Notes any content that:

- Is **illegal** under the laws of the United States or the customer's jurisdiction.
- Constitutes **child sexual abuse material (CSAM)**. Acme Notes reports CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to law enforcement without notice to the customer.
- Distributes **malware**, viruses, ransomware, or other malicious code.
- Infringes on the **intellectual property rights** of any third party, including copyrighted text, images, software, or trademarks.
- Constitutes **harassment**, threats, hate speech, or targeted intimidation of any individual or group.

## Prohibited Activities

Customers may not use Acme Notes to:

- Perform **automated scraping** or bulk extraction of content outside the published API, or in excess of documented rate limits.
- **Reverse engineer**, decompile, or disassemble any part of the Acme Notes client or service, except where such restriction is prohibited by applicable law.
- **Share account credentials** with any person other than the registered account holder. Each user requires their own seat.
- **Resell, sublicense, or rebrand** the Acme Notes service without an executed Acme Notes Partnership Agreement.
- **Mine cryptocurrency**, run distributed compute workloads, or otherwise consume shared infrastructure for purposes unrelated to note-taking and collaboration.
- Attempt to gain **unauthorized access** to other customers' workspaces, accounts, or data.
- Send **unsolicited bulk messages** or use Acme Notes as a vehicle for spam.

## Enforcement

Acme Notes enforces this AUP using a tiered approach. Severity determines the starting tier; severe or repeated violations may skip tiers.

1. **Warning** — written notice to the account owner with a description of the violation and a required remediation window (typically 7 days).
2. **7-day suspension** — temporary loss of access to the affected workspace. Data is preserved during suspension.
3. **Permanent ban** — termination of the account and removal of access. Reserved for severe or repeated violations, including any CSAM, malware distribution, or attempts to compromise the service.

Acme Notes may **immediately suspend or terminate** an account without prior warning where required by law or where continued access poses a material risk to other customers or to the service.

## Appeals

A customer who believes an enforcement action was taken in error may appeal by emailing **appeals@acmenotes.example** within **30 days** of the action. Appeals must include the affected account email, the date of the action, and a written explanation. Acme Notes will issue a written decision within **10 business days** of receiving a complete appeal. Appeal decisions are final.

## Reservation of Rights

Acme Notes reserves the right to investigate any suspected violation of this AUP, including reviewing account contents to the extent necessary, and to cooperate with law enforcement and regulators. Nothing in this AUP limits any other right or remedy available to Acme Notes under contract or applicable law.

## Governing Law

This AUP is governed by the laws of the **State of Delaware**, United States, without regard to its conflict-of-law principles. Disputes arising from this AUP shall be resolved exclusively in the state and federal courts located in Delaware.

## Updates

Acme Notes may update this AUP from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by **email to account owners at least 30 days before the changes take effect**. Continued use of the service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated AUP.
