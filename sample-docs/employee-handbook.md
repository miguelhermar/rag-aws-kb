# Acme Notes Employee Handbook

This handbook summarizes the most frequently referenced people-operations policies at Acme Notes. It applies to all full-time employees globally unless local law requires otherwise.

## Paid Time Off

Acme Notes provides accrued PTO based on tenure:

- **Years 1-2**: **15 days per year**, accrued monthly.
- **Years 3-5**: **20 days per year**.
- **Years 6 and beyond**: **25 days per year**.

Accrued PTO is capped at **1.5x the annual accrual rate**. Once the cap is reached, no additional PTO accrues until the balance is drawn down. Unused PTO above the cap is not paid out except at separation, where local law requires payout.

PTO requests should be submitted in the HRIS at least **2 weeks in advance** for absences of 3 days or more. Shorter absences require manager notification only.

## Parental Leave

All new parents — birthing, non-birthing, and adoptive — are eligible for **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave**, to be taken within the **first 12 months** following the birth or placement of the child. Leave may be taken continuously or in two blocks with manager coordination. Parental leave does not reduce PTO accrual.

## Remote Work

Acme Notes is a **fully remote** company. Employees may work from any location where Acme Notes is registered as an employer, subject to the following requirements:

- A minimum **4-hour daily overlap** with the employee's assigned team timezone is required.
- Employees must maintain a private, reasonably quiet workspace suitable for video calls.
- All work must be performed on company-issued equipment in compliance with the Information Security Policy.
- A **quarterly in-person offsite** (typically 3 days) is required. Travel is fully reimbursed under the Travel & Expense policy.

## Expense Reimbursement

### Meals (while traveling for work)

- **Domestic travel**: up to **$50 per day**.
- **International travel**: up to **$75 per day**.

Alcohol is not reimbursable except at pre-approved team events. Itemized receipts are required for any single expense over $25.

### Home Office

Each new employee receives a one-time **$1,000 home office stipend** within their first 90 days. The stipend may be used for desk, chair, monitor, lighting, and other ergonomic equipment. Receipts must be submitted within 6 months of hire.

### Co-Working Space

Employees who prefer to work from a co-working space may be reimbursed up to **$300 per month** with prior manager approval. Approval is renewed annually.

### Approval Workflow

- Expenses **up to $500**: manager approval only.
- Expenses **over $500**: require **both manager and Finance approval** before the expense is incurred where practical, and always before reimbursement is issued.
- All expenses must be submitted in the expense tool within **30 days** of the transaction date. Expenses submitted after 60 days are not reimbursable absent extenuating circumstances.

## Working Hours and Availability

Acme Notes does not enforce fixed working hours beyond the timezone overlap requirement. Employees are trusted to manage their own schedules and deliver against their commitments. Sustained unavailability without coverage should be communicated to the manager in advance.
