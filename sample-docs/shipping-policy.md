# Acme Notes Shipping Policy

Acme Notes ships physical accessories under the **Acme Notebook** hardware line, including branded paper notebooks, sticker packs, and USB drives preloaded with template bundles. This policy describes shipping methods, timelines, and customer responsibilities.

## Domestic Shipping (United States)

- **Carriers**: USPS Priority Mail and UPS Ground.
- **Delivery time**: **3-5 business days** from the date the order ships, excluding weekends and federal holidays.
- **Cut-off**: Orders placed before **2:00 PM Pacific Time** on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after the cut-off ship the next business day.
- **Free shipping threshold**: Orders with a merchandise subtotal of **$75 or more** ship free via USPS Priority Mail. Below the threshold, flat shipping of $6.95 applies.

## International Shipping

- **Carrier**: DHL Express for all international destinations.
- **Delivery time**: **7-21 business days** depending on destination and customs processing.
- **Coverage**: Acme Notes ships to **40+ countries**, including Canada, Mexico, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, France, the Netherlands, Spain, Italy, Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Switzerland, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Singapore, South Korea, Brazil, and the United Arab Emirates. A full current list is available at checkout.
- **Customs and duties**: Import duties, taxes, and customs fees are the **responsibility of the recipient** and are not included in the purchase price or shipping fee. DHL will contact the recipient directly if payment is required before delivery.

## Tracking

A tracking link is delivered by email to the address on the order within **24 hours** of the package leaving the Acme Notes warehouse. Tracking updates are provided by the carrier and may lag actual package movement by several hours. If no tracking email arrives within 3 business days of order confirmation, contact **support@acmenotes.example**.

## Lost or Damaged Packages

1. If a package is marked delivered but not received, wait **48 hours** before reporting — carriers occasionally scan packages as delivered before the final handoff.
2. After 48 hours, email **support@acmenotes.example** with the order ID and tracking number.
3. Acme Notes will open a carrier trace, which typically resolves within **5-10 business days**.
4. If the carrier confirms the package is lost, Acme Notes will ship a **replacement at no cost** or issue a full refund at the customer's choice.
5. Damaged packages must be reported within **7 days** of delivery with photographs of the packaging and contents.

## Returns of Hardware

Unopened hardware may be returned within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund. The customer is responsible for return shipping unless the item was defective or shipped in error. Opened hardware is not eligible for return (see the Refund Policy for related rules).

## Address Accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing a correct and complete shipping address at checkout. Packages returned to Acme Notes due to an incorrect address may be reshipped at the customer's expense or refunded less the original shipping cost.
