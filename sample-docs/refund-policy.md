# Acme Notes Refund Policy

This policy governs refunds for paid Acme Notes subscriptions and related purchases. It applies to all customers transacting directly with Acme Notes. Customers on Enterprise contracts should refer to their Master Services Agreement first; this policy applies only where the MSA is silent.

## Eligibility Windows

- **Monthly plans**: Full refunds are available within **30 days** of the most recent charge, provided the account has not exceeded fair-use thresholds during the billing period.
- **Annual plans**: Refunds are available within **14 days** of the most recent renewal and are **prorated** based on the number of days the subscription was active in the current billing year.
- **Enterprise contracts**: Refunds are bound by the terms of the executed contract. Mid-term cancellations generally do not entitle the customer to a refund unless explicitly negotiated.

## Non-Refundable Items

The following purchases are **not eligible** for refund under any tier:

- Add-on storage purchased separately from a base subscription
- One-time template marketplace purchases after download
- Hardware accessories that have been opened or used (see the Shipping Policy for return shipping rules)
- Third-party integration fees billed by partners

## How to Request a Refund

1. Email **billing@acmenotes.example** from the address registered to the account.
2. Include the **order ID** (format `AN-YYYYMMDD-XXXXX`), the date of the charge, and a brief reason for the refund request.
3. The billing team will acknowledge the request within **2 business days** and confirm eligibility.
4. Approved refunds are issued to the **original payment method**. Card refunds typically take **5-7 business days** to appear on the customer statement; bank transfer refunds may take up to **10 business days**.
5. If the original payment method is no longer valid, the customer must provide alternative bank details and proof of identity before the refund can be reissued.

## Escalation Path

If a refund request is denied and the customer believes the decision is incorrect, the request may be escalated as follows:

1. Reply to the original billing thread and request escalation to the **Billing Operations Manager**.
2. If unresolved within **5 business days**, escalation may proceed to the **Director of Customer Operations** via the same thread.
3. Final disputes that cannot be resolved internally may be referred to the chargeback process with the customer's card issuer; Acme Notes will respond to chargebacks with documentation of the original purchase and prior correspondence.

## Currency and Taxes

Refunds are issued in the **original transaction currency**. Where applicable, sales tax and VAT are refunded in proportion to the refunded amount. Currency conversion losses caused by exchange rate fluctuations between the charge and the refund are not reimbursed by Acme Notes.

## Fraud and Abuse

Acme Notes reserves the right to deny refund requests on accounts that show evidence of policy abuse, including repeated purchase-and-refund cycles, chargeback abuse, or violations of the Acceptable Use Policy.
